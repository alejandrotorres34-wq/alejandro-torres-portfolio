Project 01

Variable-Ramp Supersonic / Hypersonic Inlet CFD & FEA Study

Role: CAD Modeling, CFD Setup, FEA Setup · Tools: SolidWorks, AutoCAD, ANSYS Fluent, ANSYS Mechanical

Designed and analyzed a variable-ramp inlet concept for high-speed propulsion. The study connected geometry setup, meshing, pressure behavior, and structural response to evaluate design risk areas.

  • Modeled inlet body, movable ramp, throat/isolator, diffuser duct, shaft, side brackets, and fasteners.
  • Built a preliminary Mach 6 CFD workflow to examine shock behavior, pressure trends, and possible separation areas.
  • Used pressure-based loading assumptions in static structural analysis to study deformation and stress concentrations.
